## Collection page best practices
- One editorial hero image per collection (model in motion, not flat lay).
- Short, sensory intro (≤ 2 lines) — never a keyword dump above the fold.
- Filters: Era · Category · Size · Colour · Price · Length.
- Add a **collection footer block** of 80–120 words of SEO prose *below* the grid (keyword-rich, human-written) for ranking without hurting the editorial feel.
- Cross-link to 2–3 related collections at the bottom ("Also in your vacation era →").

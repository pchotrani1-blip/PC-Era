# 09 · Packaging — Collectible Unboxing

The unboxing is the brand's first physical handshake and a content moment. Everything butter-yellow, cream, gold-foil, tactile, *collectible*.

## System overview
| Item | Material / colour | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **Mailer / courier bag** | Recyclable matte mailer, soft black or kraft | Champagne-gold `PC ERA` wordmark, "The Era of Trend" + monogram pattern interior |
| **Gift box** | Rigid butter-yellow box, magnetic close | Blind-deboss `PC` monogram on lid; cream interior |
| **Tissue paper** | Cream tissue, butter monogram repeat | Sealed with a gold foil `PC` sticker |
| **Dust bag** | Cotton/canvas, warm beige | Screen-printed wordmark; reusable, keeps the collectible feel |
| **Thank-you card** | Heavy cream stock, letterpress | "Welcome to your era." + handwritten-style note |
| **Greeting card (gift)** | Folded cream card | Blank inside for gift messages, butter envelope |
| **Stickers** | Die-cut, butter + gold | Monogram seal + "In My PC Era" + era words |
| **Swing tags** | Thick cream card, gold thread | Front: monogram + price; back: era story + care + QR to styling |
| **Shopping bag (retail/pop-up)** | Rigid butter paper bag, cotton-rope handle | Wordmark front, monogram pattern, gold foil |
| **Care card** | Small cream card | Fabric care + "made to be kept, not replaced" |

## Unboxing sequence (designed to film well)
1. Soft-black mailer with gold wordmark arrives.
2. Inside: a **butter-yellow rigid box** — already feels like a gift.
3. Lift the lid (magnetic *thunk*) → cream tissue + gold seal.
4. Garment in a beige **dust bag**.
5. On top: **thank-you card** ("Welcome to your era."), care card, sticker sheet.
6. Swing tag with QR → "How to style your piece" landing page.

## Inserts that drive repeat & UGC
- **Discount card:** "Your next era — 10% off, code on the back."
- **Referral card:** "Give a friend 15%, get 15%."
- **UGC prompt:** "Tag @wearpcera & #InMyPCEra — be featured & earn 50 points."
- **QR styling card:** links to the product's "complete the look".

## Sustainability notes
- Recyclable/FSC-certified papers, soy inks, no plastic poly bags (use compostable if needed).
- Reusable dust bag & box reframe packaging as *keepable*, reducing waste perception.
- State it quietly on the care card — "considered, and made to last".

## Brand consistency
- Only butter, cream, beige, soft black, champagne foil. No other colours.
- Wordmark letter-spaced 0.22em; monogram in thin gold circle.
- Tactile finishes (deboss, foil, letterpress, uncoated stock) > glossy print.

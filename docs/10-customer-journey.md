# 10 · Customer Journey

> Instagram → Website → Product Page → Cart → Checkout → Unboxing → Wearing → Sharing → Repeat.

## 1. Discovery — Instagram / Pinterest / TikTok
She sees a Reel: "the affordable House of CB linen set". The aesthetic stops her scroll — cream, butter, European summer. She taps the profile, sees a cohesive magazine-grid, reads "In My PC Era". She taps the link in bio.
**Brand job:** consistent aesthetic, era-led content, frictionless link-in-bio to the exact product.

## 2. Landing — Homepage
Hero loads fast, image pans gently, "The Era of Trend". She scrolls through best sellers, the era marquee, the founder story, real reviews, UGC. It feels expensive but warm — not intimidating. A subtle popup offers 10% off her first chapter.
**Brand job:** instant luxury feeling, social proof early, email capture, clear "Shop new in".

## 3. Consideration — Product page
She lands on the Amalfi Set. Big editorial gallery, zoom on the fabric. "In your vacation era." She reads *why she'll love it* and *made for every era*. Model measurements + size guide answer her fit anxiety. Reviews with photos reassure. "Selling fast — a few left in your size" nudges her. Sticky add-to-bag follows on mobile.
**Brand job:** remove fit/quality doubt, sell the feeling, urgency + social proof, easy add.

## 4. Cart — Drawer
Adds to bag → drawer slides in. "You're $22 away from complimentary shipping" → she adds the matching top to qualify (cross-sell). Trust line: easy 30-day returns. Afterpay shown.
**Brand job:** raise AOV (free-ship threshold + complete-the-look), reduce risk, keep momentum.

## 5. Checkout
Shop Pay one-tap, or express (Apple/Google Pay). Clean, on-brand, fast. Afterpay/Klarna for 4 payments. Order confirmation feels warm, not robotic, and teases the butter-yellow box.
**Brand job:** zero friction, accelerated wallets, reassurance, brand voice even here.

## 6. Anticipation — Post-purchase emails
"It's official ✦", then "Your era is on its way" with tracking and a styling tip. She's earning loyalty points. Excitement builds.
**Brand job:** keep her warm, set up the unboxing, seed loyalty + review.

## 7. Unboxing
Soft-black mailer → butter-yellow box → cream tissue + gold seal → dust bag → thank-you card → sticker sheet → QR styling card. It feels like a gift she bought herself. She instinctively films it.
**Brand job:** collectible, film-worthy, prompt UGC, reinforce premium perception.

## 8. Wearing
The fit is true, the fabric photographs like luxury. She wears it to brunch; someone asks where it's from. The garment overdelivers vs. price.
**Brand job:** product quality must exceed the price tag — this is the whole flywheel.

## 9. Sharing
She tags @wearpcera, #InMyPCEra, earns 50 points, gets reposted, feels seen. Her friends ask; she shares her 15% referral code.
**Brand job:** make sharing rewarding and easy; turn customers into the campaign.

## 10. Repeat
Review request → points → "Back in stock" and "Summer ’26" drops → she's now in a new era (corporate, date night). VIP tier unlocks early access. She returns not for a discount, but because PC Era dresses *every version of her*.
**Brand job:** segment by era, retain via loyalty + relevance, grow LTV.

# 08 · Photography & Art Direction

The visual engine of the brand. Every image should feel like a page torn from a European summer magazine.

## North star
Jacquemus sun + Deiji Studios calm + Djerf Avenue warmth + The Row restraint. Editorial, warm, expensive, effortless.

## Locations
- **Hero / campaign:** sun-washed terraces, stone villas, Amalfi/Mykonos/Mallorca coastlines, olive groves, marble cafés, linen-draped interiors.
- **Everyday / studio:** warm ivory cyclorama, beige paper, sheer-curtain window light, terracotta floors.
- **Lifestyle:** coffee tables, vintage cars, pilates studios, hotel pools, market flowers, train windows.

## Lighting
- **Primary:** natural light, golden hour or soft midday diffused through linen/sheer.
- **Studio:** one large soft source (5ft octa/scrim) + bounce; soft, wrapping shadows — never hard flash.
- Embrace warm sun flare and gentle shadow play (palm fronds, window mullions).

## Styling & props
- Gold jewellery, raffia bags, strappy sandals, silk scarves, sunglasses.
- Espresso cups, fresh figs, lemons, white wine, books, linen napkins, fresh flowers.
- Keep props minimal and tonal — they support, never distract.
- Hair: effortless — slicked buns, soft waves, air-dry texture. Makeup: glowy, "your skin but better".

## Camera settings (starting points)
- **Editorial portrait:** 50mm or 85mm, f/2–f/2.8, ISO 100–200, 1/500s+ in sun. Shallow but not gimmicky depth.
- **Full look / motion:** 35mm, f/4–f/5.6 for sharpness head-to-toe; walking/twirling frames for the "movement" the site wants.
- **Detail/fabric:** 100mm macro, f/4, show weave and drape.
- Shoot RAW, slight overexpose (+0.3–0.7) for that bright, airy, creamy base.

## Poses & direction
- Movement over stiffness: walking, turning, adjusting a strap, laughing mid-step, hand in hair, looking off-frame.
- Candid > posed. "In-between" moments. Negative space around the subject for editorial crops.
- Show the garment in life: sitting at a café, stepping into a car, on a terrace.

## Editing & colour grade
- Warm white balance (5500–6500K), lifted shadows, gentle highlight roll-off.
- Desaturate greens/blues slightly; protect skin tones; nudge overall toward cream/butter.
- Low contrast, soft film-like grain, no heavy clarity. Consistent LUT across the whole shoot.
- Export: WebP for web (≤200KB), sRGB; keep a high-res TIFF master.

## Shot list per product (minimum)
1. Front-on full look (clean bg) — also the Shopping feed image.
2. Back view.
3. 3/4 movement / walking.
4. Seated / lifestyle in context.
5. Fabric/detail macro.
6. Styling alt (different era).
7. Optional 5–8s motion clip for product gallery + Reels.

- **"Every Version of Her":** one muse, six eras in six setups, single location, palette-consistent.
- **"In My PC Era":** documentary-style, six real girls, hand-held film + portraits.

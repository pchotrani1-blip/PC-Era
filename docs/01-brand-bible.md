# 01 · PC Era Brand Bible

## The one line
**PC Era — The Era of Trend.** Affordable luxury, made to dress every version of her.

## Mission
PC Era is an affordable luxury women's fashion brand. Every girl goes through different eras of life — confident, soft girl, vacation, corporate, date night, brunch, healing, main character. PC Era exists to dress *every version of her*. Fashion shouldn't only be saved for occasions; it should make everyday moments feel elevated.

## The eras (our content & merchandising engine)
Each era is a mood, a collection lens, an email segment, and a content pillar.

| Era | Feeling | Wears |
|---|---|---|
| Confident era | Powerful, sharp, certain | Tailoring, column dresses, sets |
| Soft girl era | Tender, romantic, quiet | Knits, slip skirts, pastels |
| Vacation era | Sun, salt, freedom | Linen, crochet, resort, maxi |
| Corporate era | Composed, expensive | Blazers, trousers, shirting |
| Date night era | Magnetic, deliberate | Mini dresses, satin, bodycon |
| Brunch era | Easy, pretty, social | Matching sets, sundresses |
| Healing era | Gentle, restful | Loungewear, oversized, neutrals |
| Main character era | Cinematic, bold | Statement dresses, the It-piece |

## Brand feeling (the non-negotiables)
Luxurious · minimal · editorial · expensive · clean · fashion-forward · soft femininity · European summer · Pinterest aesthetic · quiet luxury · timeless · high-end *without intimidation*.

## Customer
Women 18–35. Lives on Instagram, Pinterest, TikTok. Shops Zara, Source Unknown, House of CB, Meshki, Princess Polly, DISSH, Mango, COS. Loves vacations, coffee dates, pilates, travel, brunch, fashion reels, moodboards. She wants clothes that look designer but stay affordable.

## Voice & tone
- **Editorial, never corporate.** Short lines. Confident. A little poetic.
- Speaks *to her*, like a stylish friend with taste — never salesy.
- Uses the "era" language naturally ("In your vacation era.").
- Sentence fragments are welcome. White space in copy = white space on the page.
- **Avoid:** exclamation spam, "Buy now!!!", jargon, hype, discount-brand energy.
- **Reach for:** "made for", "the era of", "quietly", "becoming", "every version of her".

### Voice swaps
| Don't say | Say |
|---|---|
| "Shop our amazing sale now!" | "Last chance pieces. Quietly marked down." |
| "Best quality guaranteed" | "Made to be kept, not replaced." |
| "Sign up for our newsletter" | "Join the era — 10% off your first chapter." |
| "Add to cart" | "Add to bag" |

## Typography
- **Headlines:** luxury serif — **Canela** (production) / Fraunces (fallback). Large, tight leading, slight negative tracking.
- **Accents:** editorial **italic** serif for one-word emphasis ("*era*", "*becoming*").
- **Body:** modern sans — **Neue Haas Grotesk** (production) / Inter. Light-to-regular weight, generous line-height (1.65).
- **Eyebrows / labels:** sans, uppercase, wide tracking (0.18em).

## Logo / wordmark
- Primary: **PC ERA** wordmark, serif, letter-spaced 0.22em, uppercase.
- Monogram: **PC** in a thin champagne circle for favicons, swing tags, dust bags.
- Clear space = cap height on all sides. Never stretch, never add shadow, never place on busy imagery without a scrim.

## Look & layout principles
Large whitespace · editorial photography · very little clutter · large luxurious fonts · slow luxury · magazine layout · soft animations · micro-interactions · hover animations · luxury scrolling · beautiful transitions. Looks expensive on desktop **and** mobile.

## Brand values
1. **Every era, dressed beautifully** — versatility is luxury.
2. **Considered quality** — premium fabric, honest pricing.
3. **Elevated everyday** — not saved for occasions.
4. **She comes first** — easy returns, real care, collectible unboxing.
5. **Quietly confident** — we whisper; we never shout.

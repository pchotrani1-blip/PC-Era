# 06 · Email & SMS Flows (luxury, editorial)

Platform: **Klaviyo** (recommended). Design language: cream background, serif headlines, one editorial image per email, butter-yellow CTA buttons on ink, lots of whitespace. Keep copy short.

Global signature footer: *In your PC Era — @wearpcera · Unsubscribe · Manage preferences.*

---

## 1. Welcome / 10% off (3 emails)
**Trigger:** newsletter or popup signup.
- **Email 1 — instant (subject:** "Welcome to your era ✦ here's 10% off")
  Hero: brand image. Copy: "You're in. Every girl moves through eras — we're here to dress every one of them. Here's 10% off your first chapter." CTA: *Shop new in* + code `ERA10`.
- **Email 2 — +24h (subject:** "Which era are you in?")
  Editorial grid linking the 4 era collections. Copy: "Confident, soft, vacation, main character — find yours." CTA: *Find your era*.
- **Email 3 — +72h (subject:** "Still thinking it over?")
  Social proof: 3 reviews + best sellers. Reminder of `ERA10` expiring. CTA: *Shop best sellers*.

## 2. Abandoned cart (3 emails)
- **+1h (subject:** "You left something beautiful behind")
  Show cart items, large imagery. Copy: "Your next era is waiting in your bag." CTA: *Return to bag*.
- **+24h (subject:** "Still in your bag ✦")
  Add urgency + reviews for the item. "Selling fast — and almost out in your size." CTA: *Complete checkout*.
- **+48h (subject:** "A little something to help you decide")
  Offer 10% (`ERA10`) if not already used. CTA: *Take 10% off*.

## 3. Browse abandonment (1 email)
- **+4h (subject:** "Still thinking about the {product}?")
  Single product hero + "complete the look" trio. CTA: *Shop the look*.

## 4. Order confirmation (transactional, elevated)
- **Subject:** "It's official — your PC Era order is in ✦"
  Warm thank-you, order summary, "what happens next", unboxing tease ("Your butter-yellow box is being packed by hand"). Cross-sell: "Complete the set →". Loyalty: "You just earned {x} points."

## 5. Shipping (2 emails)
- **Dispatched (subject:** "Your era is on its way")
  Tracking button, est. delivery, styling tip for the piece they bought.
- **Delivered (subject:** "It's here — show us your era")
  Unboxing prompt: "Tag @wearpcera & #InMyPCEra to be featured." Review request seed.

## 6. Review request (1–2 emails)
- **+7 days post-delivery (subject:** "How's your {product}?")
  One-click star rating, photo upload incentive ("+50 points & a chance to be featured"). CTA: *Leave a review*.

## 7. VIP / loyalty (segment flow)
- **Tier reached (subject:** "Welcome to the inner circle ✦")
  Early access, free express, birthday gift, points balance. Quarterly "VIP private edit" email.

## 8. Back-in-stock (triggered)
- **Subject:** "Back, by popular demand ✦ {product} restocked"
  Single hero, urgency ("it sold out once"). CTA: *Shop before it's gone*.

## 9. Launch / new drop (campaign, not flow)
- **Teaser (−48h):** "Something's coming. Summer ’26." countdown.
- **Launch (0h):** "Summer ’26 is here." Full editorial, shop CTA.
- **Last chance (+5d):** "Almost gone — Summer ’26 best sellers."

## 10. Birthday (annual)
- **Subject:** "Happy birthday, gorgeous ✦ a gift in your inbox"
  15% off or free gift code, 7-day expiry. Collect birthday at signup.

---

## SMS (optional, sparing)
- Welcome: "Welcome to PC Era ✦ 10% off: ERA10. Reply STOP to opt out."
- Abandoned cart: "Your bag misses you — checkout here {link}."
- Drop: "Summer ’26 just dropped. Shop before it sells out {link}."

## Design checklist
- 600px width, single column, mobile-first.
- One serif headline, ≤ 40 words body, one CTA per fold.
- Butter CTA on ink or ink CTA on cream — high contrast, ~48px tall.
- Real photography, never stock. Alt text on every image.

# 07 · Social Media Strategy

The feed and the website must feel like one world: cream, butter, editorial, European-summer, Pinterest-coded.

## Instagram

### Grid aesthetic
- Cohesive warm-neutral palette: cream, butter, beige, sand, soft black.
- Alternate **rhythm**: editorial campaign → product flat-lay-in-context → UGC/selfie → text-on-cream quote → lifestyle (coffee/travel) → repeat.
- Consistent grading (see `08-photography-direction.md`): warm, slightly lifted blacks, low saturation, creamy highlights.
- Every 9-grid should read like a magazine spread, not a catalogue.

### Content pillars (the eras)
1. **Product as feeling** — "In your vacation era" carousels.
2. **Editorial campaigns** — seasonal hero shoots.
3. **UGC / community** — #InMyPCEra reposts.
4. **Styling** — "3 ways to wear the Amalfi Set".
5. **Brand world** — coffee, travel, pilates, Pinterest moodboards, BTS.
6. **Social proof** — review screenshots on cream.

### Posting cadence
- 4–5 feed posts/week, 5–7 Stories/day, 4–5 Reels/week, Broadcast channel for drops.

### Captions (voice)
- Short, lowercase-leaning, editorial. "in her vacation era ✦" / "the set you'll live in this summer."
- 1 line + a soft CTA ("shop via the link in bio") + 3–5 niche hashtags, not 30.
- Hashtag set: #InMyPCEra #affordableluxury #quietluxury #europeansummer #matchingset #ootd #pintereststyle

### Story highlights (covers: butter circle + thin serif label)
`New In` · `Shop` · `Eras` · `Reviews` · `Unboxing` · `Sizing` · `Behind PC Era` · `Travel` · `FAQ`

### Launch countdown (drop playbook)
- **−7 days:** moodboard teaser post + "something's coming" Story poll.
- **−3 days:** fabric/detail close-ups, countdown sticker.
- **−24h:** full look reveal silhouette, "set your reminder".
- **Drop day:** Reel walk-through + carousel + broadcast + Story countdown to live.
- **+48h:** UGC of first buyers + "selling fast".

### Reels (4–5/wk)
- Get-ready / "styling one set 3 ways" / packing for Europe / unboxing ASMR / "POV: your main character era" / trending-audio outfit transitions.
- Hook in first 1s (movement or a bold on-screen line). On-screen text in brand serif. 7–15s sweet spot.

## Pinterest
- Treat as a search engine + lookbook. Boards: "Vacation Era", "Matching Sets", "European Summer", "Quiet Luxury Outfits", "Date Night".
- Vertical 2:3 pins, keyword-rich titles + descriptions ("affordable linen matching set — vacation outfit").
- Pin every product + every Journal article (rich pins enabled). 5–10 pins/day.
- This is the #1 traffic driver for this aesthetic — invest here.

## TikTok
- Native, less polished, more face-to-camera: "I found the affordable House of CB", "get ready with me", "is this the prettiest linen set?", hauls, sizing honesty.
- Lean into trends + sounds; reuse Reels but reshoot hooks natively.

## Campaign ideas
- **"In My PC Era"** — film 6 girls, each in a different era (confident, soft, vacation…), one set each. Anthem video + 6 stills.
- **"The Era of Summer"** — Amalfi/European-summer campaign, terrace + linen + espresso.
- **"Every Version of Her"** — one model, six eras, six looks, one carousel.
